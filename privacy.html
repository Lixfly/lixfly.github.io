Privacy Policy

Trend-to-Alpha Archive is used only by its owner.

  • It requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, which allows access only to files this app creates.
  • It does not read other Drive files, and does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
